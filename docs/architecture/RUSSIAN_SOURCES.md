# Russian psychology sources review

Reviewed on 2026-10-03. This is a small, topic-specific first expansion, not an
exhaustive or quality-ranked bibliography. A source being authored in Russia,
published in Russian, or written by an established expert does not by itself
make every statement in it current empirical evidence.

## Passages eligible for retrieval

### Bashutina, Chernov, and Kalchinskaya (2025)

“Взаимосвязь показателей эмоционального интеллекта и удовлетворенности
романтическими отношениями у партнеров молодого возраста.” _Cifra. Психология_,
1(6), 2025. DOI: [10.60797/PSY.2025.6.1](https://doi.org/10.60797/PSY.2025.6.1).
The publisher identifies the publication as peer reviewed, lists all three
authors at Pirogov Russian National Research Medical University, and marks this
article CC BY 4.0. Two short, attributed Russian passages are included in the
seed.

Limit: the study reports 22 student couples (44 participants) and correlational
analyses. Treat results as exploratory and population-specific; do not infer
causation or generalize to all couples. The article includes broader causal
claims in its discussion that are not imported as evidence chunks.

## Retrieval and rights rules

- Reusable CC BY and permission-backed passages store the source's actual
  license, locator, canonical attribution, and permission provenance where
  applicable.
- The four expert books remain metadata-only: no authorized book text files
  were available locally to extract passages from. They do not yet increase
  semantic retrieval coverage.
- A metadata-only source has no embedding and cannot support an AI verdict.
- Three Russian articles now add one passage each. The books expand the
  bibliography, not retrieval coverage.
- Migration `20261003100000_russian_bibliography_sources.sql`, the expanded
  seed, and all 33 current embeddings were applied and verified in linked
  Production on 2026-10-03.
